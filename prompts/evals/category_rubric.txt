{message}

{analysis.category}

Evaluate whether the assigned category correctly represents

0.0 = Category is clearly incorrect.

Rules:
- Evaluate the primary customer problem, not incidental details.
- Do not penalize differences in wording if the underlying meaning
  is equivalent.
- If the message is ambiguous, reduce the score rather than assuming
  information that is not present.
